# Team brief — <debate id>

Written for the team, in the human's language. One line first.

**Conclusion:** <one line>

1. **What was being decided** and what was not.
2. **The criteria** and their weights.
3. **The options** considered.
4. **The weighty reasons** for the result.
5. **The defense:** the strongest case for the losing side, and why it lost.
6. **What was decided** (decision IDs) and what waits for the human.
7. **Minorities:** who disagreed and what would change their mind.
8. **Next steps** and the gates that could reverse the decision.
9. **Why it can be trusted:** rounds, independent and fresh panels, model
   families, lead checks, cross-review.
